About Shinri

In anime lore and Japanese philosophy, Shinri (真理) translates to "The Ultimate Truth" — reality stripped of speculation and illusion. Applied to finance, Shinri is an evidence-grounded Mutual Funds Facts Chatbot engineered for the Groww mutual fund ecosystem. Instead of generic AI chatbots that hallucinate numbers or provide unauthorized financial advice, Shinri queries verified statutory scheme disclosures (SID, KIM, and factsheets) to deliver verifiable, source-attributed financial facts.
