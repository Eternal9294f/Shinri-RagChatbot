SECTION: Overview & Objective
HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Plan - Growth is an open-ended equity mutual fund scheme offered by HDFC Mutual Fund. The investment objective is to generate long-term capital appreciation by predominantly investing in equity and equity-related instruments of large-cap companies. It is categorized under Equity: Large Cap. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Expense Ratio & Charges
The expense ratio of HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Plan - Growth is 0.94% (inclusive of GST). The expense ratio represents the annual percentage fee charged by the fund management company to manage the fund. A stamp duty of 0.005% is applicable on all mutual fund purchases from July 1, 2020. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Exit Load
The exit load for HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Plan - Growth is 1.00% if redeemed or switched out within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment. If redeemed after 1 year (365 days), the exit load is Nil (0%). Exit load is a charge levied on investors if they redeem their units before the specified holding period. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Minimum Investment & SIP
For HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Plan - Growth, the minimum SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) amount is Rs 100 per month. The minimum one-time lumpsum investment amount is Rs 100. There is no upper limit on the maximum investment amount. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Lock-in Period & Redemption
HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Plan - Growth has Nil lock-in period. Investors can redeem their mutual fund units on any business day, subject to applicable exit loads and taxation rules. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Benchmark & Riskometer
The benchmark index for HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Plan - Growth is NIFTY 100 Total Return Index (TRI). According to the SEBI riskometer, the risk level of this fund is categorized as Very High Risk. Investors must understand that their principal will be at very high risk. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Taxation Rules
Mutual fund investments in HDFC Large Cap Fund are classified as equity-oriented for tax purposes. For units held for more than 12 months, Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) are taxed at 12.5% on gains exceeding Rs 1.25 Lakh per financial year (without indexation). For units held for 12 months or less, Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) are taxed at 20%. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund-direct-growth
