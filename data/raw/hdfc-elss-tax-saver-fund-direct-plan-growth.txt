SECTION: Overview & Objective
HDFC ELSS Tax saver Fund Direct Plan - Growth is an open-ended Equity Linked Saving Scheme (ELSS) offered by HDFC Mutual Fund. The scheme aims to generate capital appreciation while providing income tax deduction benefits under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

SECTION: Expense Ratio & Charges
The expense ratio of HDFC ELSS Tax saver Fund Direct Plan - Growth is 1.11% (inclusive of GST). Stamp duty of 0.005% applies on unit creation. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

SECTION: Exit Load
The exit load for HDFC ELSS Tax saver Fund Direct Plan - Growth is Nil (0%). Because of the statutory 3-year lock-in period, units cannot be redeemed before 3 years, and no exit load is levied when redeemed after the lock-in period expires. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

SECTION: Mandatory Lock-in Period
HDFC ELSS Tax saver Fund has a mandatory statutory lock-in period of 3 years (36 months) from the date of allotment of units. For SIP investments, each individual monthly SIP installment is locked in for 3 years from its specific allotment date. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

SECTION: Minimum Investment & SIP
For HDFC ELSS Tax saver Fund Direct Plan - Growth, the minimum monthly SIP amount is Rs 500. The minimum lumpsum investment amount is Rs 500. Investments can be made in multiples of Rs 500. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

SECTION: Tax Benefits & Section 80C
Investments in HDFC ELSS Tax saver Fund qualify for tax deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act up to a maximum limit of Rs 1.5 Lakh per financial year (under the Old Tax Regime). Under the New Tax Regime, Section 80C deductions are not available. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

SECTION: Benchmark & Riskometer
The benchmark index for HDFC ELSS Tax saver Fund Direct Plan - Growth is NIFTY 500 Total Return Index (TRI). The scheme riskometer is rated as Very High Risk. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

SECTION: Taxation on Redemption
After the 3-year lock-in period, redemption gains from HDFC ELSS Tax saver Fund are subject to Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax of 12.5% on gains exceeding Rs 1.25 Lakh in a financial year. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth

This statement serves as proof of investment for Section 80C tax deduction submission. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth
