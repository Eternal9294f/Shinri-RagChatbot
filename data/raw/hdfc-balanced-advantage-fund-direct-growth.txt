SECTION: Overview & Objective
HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Plan - Growth is an open-ended balanced advantage fund (dynamic asset allocation hybrid scheme). The scheme dynamically manages allocation between equity, arbitrage, and fixed-income debt securities depending on market valuations and macroeconomic indicators. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Expense Ratio & Charges
The expense ratio of HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Plan - Growth is 0.73% (inclusive of GST). Stamp duty of 0.005% applies on every unit allocation. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Exit Load Structure
For HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Plan - Growth: For redemption or switch-out up to 15% of units within 1 year from the date of allotment: Nil exit load. For redemption or switch-out in excess of 15% of units within 1 year from allotment: 1.00% exit load. For redemption or switch-out after 1 year from allotment: Nil (0%) exit load. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Minimum Investment & SIP
For HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Plan - Growth, the minimum monthly SIP amount is Rs 100. The minimum lumpsum investment is Rs 100, and minimum additional purchase is Rs 100. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Lock-in Period & Liquidity
HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Plan - Growth has no lock-in period. Units can be redeemed on any business day at the applicable NAV. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Benchmark & Riskometer
The benchmark for HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund Direct Plan - Growth is NIFTY 50 Hybrid Composite debt 50:50 Index. The risk level on the SEBI riskometer is rated as Very High Risk. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth

SECTION: Taxation Treatment
HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund dynamically maintains gross equity exposure (including derivatives) of at least 65%, qualifying for equity taxation. Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) over Rs 1.25 Lakh per financial year are taxed at 12.5% for units held over 12 months. Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) are taxed at 20% for units held for 12 months or less. Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-balanced-advantage-fund-direct-growth
